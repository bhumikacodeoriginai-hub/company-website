Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to making our website accessible and usable for everyone.

Our Commitment

Code Origin.AI is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards.

Standards

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities.

Measures Taken

We have implemented the following measures to ensure accessibility:

Semantic HTML structure
Keyboard navigation support
Skip navigation links
ARIA labels and roles
Sufficient color contrast
Reduced motion support
Form labels and error states
Focus-visible indicators
Responsive design for all devices
3D/WebGL fallback for assistive tech

Reduced Motion

We respect the prefers-reduced-motion media query. When this preference is enabled, animations, transitions, and 3D visualizations are minimized or disabled entirely.

Known Limitations

While we strive for full accessibility, some interactive 3D visualizations may not be fully accessible to screen readers. These elements are marked as decorative and do not contain essential content.

Feedback

We welcome feedback on the accessibility of our website. If you encounter barriers or have suggestions for improvement, please contact us at accessibility@codeorigin.ai. We aim to respond to accessibility feedback within 5 business days.

Continuous Improvement

We regularly review and update our website to improve accessibility. This includes periodic audits, user testing, and staying current with evolving accessibility standards and best practices.
